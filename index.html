Physical picture

Classically, an electron with E < V₀ cannot cross the barrier. Quantum mechanically, its wavefunction penetrates the barrier and decays exponentially.

For a rectangular barrier, κ = √(2m(V₀−E))/ħ, and the prototype uses the qualitative approximation T ≈ exp(−2κa).

Interpretation

Increase barrier height
κ increases → faster decay → smaller transmission.
Increase barrier thickness
The wave must survive a longer evanescent region → transmission falls exponentially.
