Model scope and semiconductor interpretation

Step: idealized band-offset interface. Barrier: thin tunnel barrier. Well: quantum-well region. Double barrier: idealized resonant-tunneling structure.

The present model uses one spatial dimension, piecewise-constant potentials, and the free electron mass me. It does not yet include material-dependent effective mass, self-consistent electrostatics, inelastic scattering, or many-body effects.

Wave-packet propagation approximates the whole line x ∈ ℝ with an adaptive finite computational window and absorbing edge layers. The numerical window is shown beside the playback controls.
