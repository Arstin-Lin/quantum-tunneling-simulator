Free-electron dispersion relation

For a continuum free electron, E(k)=ℏ²k²/(2me) and vg=(1/ℏ)dE/dk. During scattering, the full-state ⟨k⟩ can decrease or change sign as reflected and transmitted components coexist. The finite-difference propagator itself also has a small numerical dispersion that decreases as the spatial grid is refined.

Model scope and semiconductor interpretation

Free: reference propagation without a structure. Step: idealized band-offset interface. Barrier: thin tunnel barrier. Well: quantum-well region. Double barrier: idealized resonant-tunneling structure.

The present model uses one spatial dimension, piecewise-constant potentials, and the free electron mass me. It does not yet include material-dependent effective mass, self-consistent electrostatics, inelastic scattering, or many-body effects.

Wave-packet propagation approximates the whole line x ∈ ℝ with an adaptive finite computational window and absorbing edge layers. The numerical window is shown beside the playback controls. v1.1 additionally analyzes packet translation, spreading, and momentum content without modifying the propagator.
